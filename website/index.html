Wren is a simple, self-custody wallet for XBT, the coin of the BLAKE2b fork chain. It is being built in the open for iPhone and Android.

Stay safe

There is no Wren app to download yet. Any app, download, or person claiming to be Wren Wallet today is not us.

Wren will never ask for your recovery phrase, by message, by email, or on any website.

What Wren is being built to do

Keys stay on your phone

No accounts, no sign-up, no KYC. Wren collects no data about you. Back up with 12 words on paper.

Claim your XBT safely

Held BTC before the split at block 961,632? You may also hold XBT. Wren moves it with a hardware signer, so your BTC seed never touches a phone.

Always the right chain

Every signature is invalid on the BTC chain by design. Wren uses its own xbt: payment links and refuses BTC servers.

Private by default

Tor is built in and on from the start. Use the project server or connect your own node.

XBT is not BTC. They are separate coins on separate chains.

Built in the open

Every line of Wren is public, so anyone can check the code that holds their money. Outside bitcoin developers review the plan before code is written, again when signing works, and a formal security review comes before any release.
